Help with creating, saving and sharing your little apps.

How do I start?

Open one of the four included examples, or use Create to describe a tool or game. There's no account to set up. Cloud generation needs internet and a purchased build (top-up or Pro); direct mode can use your own API key. No coding experience is required.

Does it work offline?

Saved applets run locally without another AI request. Creating or editing with AI needs internet. Some phone capabilities depend on device support, permissions or online system services.

Why is the workshop taking time?

It builds and checks generated HTML. A request can take one to three minutes and continues in the background. With Cloud workshop enabled, you can return to the shelf later. With your own key in direct mode, keep Tinker open. If the job fails, use a focused request and retry; cloud charges for failed jobs are refunded as a build.

What does it cost?

There's no account and no free monthly allowance — cloud creation and editing are paid from the start. A creation or edit uses one build. Saved applets use none when opened. A 3-build top-up is a one-time purchase that never expires; so is an 8-build top-up. Tinker Pro provides ongoing cloud creation, revision and testing with up to 25 builds in each monthly billing period. Unused subscription allowance expires at the end of its period and does not roll over.

The US reference prices are $4.99 for 3 builds, $9.99 for 8 builds, and $19.99 per month for Pro. Your store shows the current localized price before purchase. Availability depends on the release and store configuration. Your AI provider's charges apply separately when you use your own API key.

How do I buy or restore?

Open the workshop purchase screen and buy directly — no sign-in first. After changing phones or reinstalling, use Restore Purchases to bring Pro back, including any unused builds from your Pro period. A purchase is credited after server verification, which can take a moment. A pending store payment is not a completed purchase. Top-up builds bought without Pro stay only on the device where you bought them and do not transfer with Restore Purchases; buy top-ups on the device you plan to use them on.

How do I cancel Pro?

Use Manage subscription in Tinker or your Apple subscription settings or Google Play subscriptions. Pro renews automatically until cancelled; access remains for the paid period unless the store revokes or refunds it. Deleting the app or its cloud data does not cancel renewals — cancel in the store separately. Non-expiring top-ups are separate from your subscription. See the subscription terms.

Why have I reached a request limit?

Installs with active Pro or a purchased balance can attempt up to 40 cloud jobs per UTC day. Each allowance also has a processing limit that failed builds count toward. When it is reached, builds from that allowance pause until it resets (Pro: next renewal). Your builds are not lost, and you can contact support if a top-up is affected. A separate safety limit allows 200 attempts per UTC calendar month across all installs. Failed jobs return their charged build but still count as attempts. Daily limits reset at UTC midnight. These limits protect the workshop from repeated costly failures; a paid balance is not an unlimited-generation promise.

Can I send an app to a friend?

Use Share a copy from the applet menu to export HTML. Browser support varies, and phone-only features may not work outside Tinker. The copy excludes the applet's separate saved state; data embedded in the HTML remains included.

A phone feature is unavailable

Open the applet's permissions menu and your device permission settings. A feature may require compatible hardware or a system service. You can keep using other functions when permission is denied.

Delete data or request cloud deletion

To remove a local applet, delete it from your shelf. To remove your own API key, use Settings. These actions do not automatically erase your cloud identity, builds or usage records.

Use Settings → Privacy & support to request cloud deletion — no account or sign-in is needed to do this. This removes Tinker jobs, reports, wallets and usage data, and queues RevenueCat profile deletion. It can remove access to unused builds and does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel renewals in your store first if that is your intention. Minimal hashed financial and anti-replay records remain without an automatic expiry. Operational deletion markers normally remain 30 days and pending deletions are retried.

Automatic deletion removes your device's anonymous technical identity directly — there is no password or reauthentication step, because there is no account. For purchase recovery or deletion of support messages, email nalhamzy@gmail.com. Include a purchase reference (store, product and approximate date) so we can locate the right records. Never send a password, API key, full card number or private applet files.

Job records also follow the cleanup process described in the privacy policy. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention.

Tell us what happened

For a problem or inappropriate AI output, describe the screen, your app version and what you expected. Share a screenshot only if it contains no private information. Use Settings → Privacy & support → Report AI content to send a report without leaving the app. Email support below for other help.
