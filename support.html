Help with creating, saving and sharing your little apps.

How do I start?

Open one of the four included examples, or use Create to describe a tool or game. Cloud generation needs internet, a verified email account and an available allowance; direct mode can use your own API key. No coding experience is required.

Does it work offline?

Saved applets run locally without another AI request. Creating or editing with AI needs internet. Some phone capabilities depend on device support, permissions or online system services.

Why is the workshop taking time?

It builds and checks generated HTML. A request can take several minutes. With Cloud workshop enabled, you can return to the shelf later. With your own key in direct mode, keep Tinker open. If the job fails, use a focused request and retry; cloud charges for failed jobs are refunded as credit units.

What does it cost?

After email verification, the free cloud workshop includes 15 credits each UTC calendar month. A creation or edit uses 3 credits. Saved applets use none when opened. Tinker Pro provides ongoing cloud creation, revision and testing with 60 credits per monthly billing period, enough for 20 jobs. Unused subscription allowance expires at the end of its period and does not roll over. Top-ups contain 9 or 24 credits and do not expire.

The US reference prices are $19.99 per month for Pro, $4.99 for 9 credits and $9.99 for 24 credits. Your store shows the current localized price before purchase. Availability depends on the release and store configuration. Your AI provider's charges apply separately when you use your own API key.

How do I buy or restore?

Sign in to your verified Tinker email account, then open the workshop purchase screen. After changing phones or reinstalling, use the original verified account and Restore. A purchase is credited after server verification, which can take time. A pending store payment is not a completed purchase. If syncing is delayed, leave your account signed in and try Restore later. Do not repurchase solely because a balance is temporarily unreadable.

How do I cancel Pro?

Use Manage subscription in Tinker or your Apple subscription settings or Google Play subscriptions. Pro renews automatically until cancelled; access remains for the paid period unless the store revokes or refunds it. Deleting the app or its cloud data does not cancel renewals. Non-expiring top-ups are separate from your subscription. See the subscription terms.

Why have I reached a request limit?

Verified free accounts can attempt 8 cloud jobs per UTC day; accounts with active Pro or a purchased balance can attempt 40. A separate safety limit allows 200 attempts per UTC calendar month. Failed jobs return their charged credits but still count as attempts. Daily limits reset at UTC midnight. These limits protect the workshop from repeated costly failures; a paid balance is not an unlimited-generation promise.

Can I send an app to a friend?

Use Share a copy from the applet menu to export HTML. Browser support varies, and phone-only features may not work outside Tinker. The copy excludes the applet's separate saved state; data embedded in the HTML remains included.

A phone feature is unavailable

Open the applet's permissions menu and your device permission settings. A feature may require compatible hardware or a system service. You can keep using other functions when permission is denied.

Delete data or request cloud deletion

To remove a local applet, delete it from your shelf. To remove your own API key, use Settings. These actions do not automatically erase cloud identity, credits or usage records.

Use Settings → Privacy & support to request cloud deletion. This removes Tinker jobs, reports, wallets and usage data, and queues RevenueCat profile deletion. It can remove access to unused credits and does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel renewals in your store first if that is your intention. Minimal hashed financial and anti-replay records remain without an automatic expiry. Operational deletion markers normally remain 30 days and pending deletions are retried.

Automatic deletion removes anonymous identities and verified sign-ins originally created through Tinker. Deleting a verified Tinker-created sign-in requires recent password verification and affects other Ideal Intelligence apps using that same sign-in. Pre-existing verified accounts from other studio services are preserved while their Tinker data is removed. For verified-account removal, purchase recovery or deletion of support messages, email nalhamzy@gmail.com. Include your account email or a purchase reference and the approximate date. We may need to check ownership and use by other studio services. Never send a password, API key, full card number or private applet files.

Job records also follow the cleanup process described in the privacy policy. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention.

Tell us what happened

For a problem or inappropriate AI output, describe the screen, your app version and what you expected. Share a screenshot only if it contains no private information. Use Settings → Privacy & support → Report AI content to send a report without leaving the app. Email support below for other help.
