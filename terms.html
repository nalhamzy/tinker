Terms for Tinker's cloud workshop, Pro subscription and optional top-ups.

Ideal Intelligence · Updated September 18, 2026

Using Tinker

Tinker helps adults create and run personal tools and games. There is no account, sign-up or sign-in of any kind — the app opens straight to your shelf. Saved applets run locally; AI creation and editing require internet and either a purchased build or your own provider key. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Review it before relying on it and do not use it as a substitute for professional advice or in safety-critical situations. Use content you have permission to use, and do not use the workshop to create unlawful or harmful material.

The cloud workshop and Tinker Pro

Cloud creation and editing use an anonymous technical identity that the app creates automatically on your device — no email, password or sign-in is ever requested. Cloud creation and editing are paid; there is no free monthly allowance. Each cloud creation or full-app edit uses one build. Opening a saved applet uses no build. Pro supplies an ongoing cloud creation, revision and testing service with up to 25 builds in each monthly billing period. The monthly limit resets when Pro renews and does not carry over.

Pro's US reference price is $19.99 per month. The platform store displays the actual localized price and applicable terms before you confirm. Pro automatically renews each month until you cancel through the store. We do not offer a trial or an unlimited-generation promise in this catalog.

Optional top-ups

A 3-build top-up has a US reference price of $4.99; an 8-build top-up is $9.99. Top-ups are separate one-time purchases, do not expire and remain usable after Pro cancellation. They are not cash and cannot be transferred between unrelated devices or store accounts. Store refund rights and applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

Purchases, devices and restore

Apple or Google takes payment; RevenueCat and Tinker's server verify the transaction and update access against your device's anonymous technical identity. Pending, cancelled or unverified payments do not grant builds. A brief synchronization delay can occur.

Pro is tied to your App Store or Google Play account. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling or switching phones to bring Pro back on the new device or install; restoring also carries over any unused builds from your Pro period on the previous install.

Top-up builds bought without Pro stay with Tinker on the device and install where you bought them. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes unused top-up builds, and they cannot be restored from the store afterward, because consumable purchases like these are not restorable by Apple's and Google's own purchase-restore systems. Buy top-ups on the device you plan to keep using them on.

Cancellation and deletion

Cancel through Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, or use Manage subscription in Tinker. Cancellation stops future renewals according to the store's timing and terms. The paid period remains available unless revoked or refunded.

Uninstalling Tinker or deleting cloud data does not cancel a subscription. Cloud deletion removes the wallet and can remove access to unused builds. Cancel store renewals separately. Because there is no account, cloud deletion removes your anonymous technical identity directly with no password step. The privacy policy explains this deletion, RevenueCat deletion and retained purchase-integrity records.

Workshop limits and failures

The workshop limits the amount of model work for each request and runs automatic checks. Installs with active Pro or a purchased balance may attempt up to 40 cloud jobs per UTC day; a separate 200-attempt UTC calendar-month safety limit also applies across all installs. Each allowance also has a cloud processing limit so the service stays sustainable; builds that fail count toward it. If it is reached, new builds from that allowance pause until it resets (Pro at its next renewal) while your purchased builds remain. If a top-up is affected, contact support and we will make it right. Failed jobs count toward these limits even when builds are returned. Ask for a focused app or change. A request may fail or need a smaller scope; successful output is not guaranteed. If a cloud job fails before delivering a result, the server returns its charged build under the applicable balance rules. These returned builds are not a cash refund. Store purchase refunds are handled through the relevant store, and refunded or revoked purchases may remove associated builds or access.

What you keep

Saved local applets remain usable without Pro. Local data is your responsibility to preserve; cloud shelf backup is not included. HTML exports do not include separate applet state, and phone features may not work outside Tinker. If you use your own API key, your provider's pricing and policies apply separately from Tinker's cloud builds.

Changes and contact

Future plan changes will be reflected in the app and store before purchase; store rules govern renewal-price changes and required consent. These terms do not remove rights you have under applicable consumer law. For the Apple app, Apple's standard licensed application EULA also applies.
