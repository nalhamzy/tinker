Terms for Tinker's cloud workshop, Pro subscription and optional top-ups.

Ideal Intelligence · Updated September 9, 2026

Using Tinker

Tinker helps adults create and run personal tools and games. Saved applets run locally; AI creation and editing require internet and an available allowance or your own provider key. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Review it before relying on it and do not use it as a substitute for professional advice or in safety-critical situations. Use content you have permission to use, and do not use the workshop to create unlawful or harmful material.

Free workshop and Tinker Pro

Cloud creation and editing require a verified email account. That account receives 15 free credits per UTC calendar month. Each cloud creation or full-app edit uses 3 credits. Opening a saved applet uses no generation credits. Pro supplies an ongoing cloud creation, revision and testing service with 60 credits per monthly billing period, enough for 20 jobs. Unused Pro allowance expires at the end of that period and does not roll over.

Pro's US reference price is $19.99 per month. The platform store displays the actual localized price and applicable terms before you confirm. Pro automatically renews each month until you cancel through the store. We do not offer a trial or an unlimited-generation promise in this catalog.

Optional top-ups

A 9-credit top-up has a US reference price of $4.99; a 24-credit top-up is $9.99. Top-ups are separate one-time purchases, do not expire and remain usable after Pro cancellation while the original Tinker billing account remains available. They are not cash and cannot be transferred between unrelated accounts. Store refund rights and applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

Account, verification and restore

Cloud use and purchases require a verified email account. Apple or Google takes payment; RevenueCat and Tinker's server verify the transaction and update access. Pending, cancelled or unverified payments do not grant credits. A brief synchronization delay can occur. Sign in to the original verified Tinker account and use Restore after reinstalling or changing phones. Restoring does not grant already consumed credits again. Contact support if a purchase is linked to a deleted billing account.

Cancellation and deletion

Cancel through Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, or use Manage subscription in Tinker. Cancellation stops future renewals according to the store's timing and terms. The paid period remains available unless revoked or refunded.

Uninstalling Tinker or deleting cloud data does not cancel a subscription. Cloud deletion removes the wallet and can remove access to unused credits. Cancel store renewals separately. A verified sign-in originally created through Tinker is deleted after recent password verification; this also affects another Ideal Intelligence app if it uses that same sign-in. Pre-existing shared studio sign-ins are preserved. The privacy policy explains account deletion, RevenueCat deletion and retained purchase-integrity records.

Workshop limits and failures

The workshop limits the amount of model work for each request and runs automatic checks. Verified free accounts may attempt 8 jobs per UTC day; accounts with active Pro or a purchased balance may attempt 40. A 200-attempt UTC calendar-month safety limit also applies. Failed jobs count toward these limits even when credits are returned. Ask for a focused app or change. A request may fail or need a smaller scope; successful output is not guaranteed. If a cloud job fails before delivering a result, the server returns its charged credits under the applicable balance rules. These returned credits are not a cash refund. Store purchase refunds are handled through the relevant store, and refunded or revoked purchases may remove associated credits or access.

What you keep

Saved local applets remain usable without Pro. Local data is your responsibility to preserve; cloud shelf backup is not included. HTML exports do not include separate applet state, and phone features may not work outside Tinker. If you use your own API key, your provider's pricing and policies apply separately from Tinker's cloud credits.

Changes and contact

Future plan changes will be reflected in the app and store before purchase; store rules govern renewal-price changes and required consent. These terms do not remove rights you have under applicable consumer law. For the Apple app, Apple's standard licensed application EULA also applies.
